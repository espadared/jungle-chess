## Full description

```
Jungle - also called Dou Shou Qi, or animal chess - on a proper board, against
a computer that actually plays well.

Eight animals, each stronger than the last. Elephant beats lion beats tiger,
all the way down to the rat - except the rat beats the elephant. Get one animal
into your opponent's den and you win.

A COMPUTER THAT DOESN'T GO EASY ON YOU
Four levels. Easy makes mistakes on purpose. Insane thinks for six seconds a
move and looks a dozen moves ahead. Most people lose to Hard.

PLAYS WITH NO INTERNET
The computer opponent runs entirely on your phone, so a full game works on a
plane, underground, anywhere. No connection needed, ever.

PLAY A FRIEND
Create a room, send them the four-letter code or the link, and play wherever
you both are.

FOUR TRAP RULES
The one rule nobody agrees on. Play it the classic way, or pick one of three
common house variations - including the one where an animal in a trap cannot be
touched at all, which turns the whole game into a siege.

LOOK BACK AT WHERE IT WENT WRONG
When a game ends, step back through it one move at a time and find the mistake.

ALSO
- English and Chinese
- Pieces as coloured animals, emoji, or the characters used on a real board
- Pass and play on one device
- No ads, no accounts, no sign-up, nothing collected about you
```
