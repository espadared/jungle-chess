Privacy

Jungle · Animal Chess

The short version

Jungle does not collect, store or share anything about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers of any kind. Nothing you do is tied to your name, your email or your device.

What stays on your device

Two settings are remembered in your browser or app so the game looks the way you left it: your chosen language and your chosen piece markings. If you are in the middle of an online game, the room code and that room's one-time key are kept too, so closing the app does not lose your game.

None of it leaves your device, and clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Playing against the computer

The computer opponent runs entirely on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and it works with no internet connection at all.

Playing a friend online

When you create or join a room, our server passes moves between the two players. It holds only what a game needs: the four-letter room code, the list of moves, which colour each player has, and a random key per seat so nobody else can move your pieces.

There is no name, no email and no account attached to any of it. Rooms live only in the server's memory and are erased after three hours of silence, or whenever the server restarts. Nothing is written to a database, and there is no game history kept anywhere.

As with any website, our host records ordinary connection logs. We do not use them for anything and we do not combine them with anything.

Children

Jungle is suitable for all ages. Because it collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, the new version will appear on this page. Questions can go to the address listed on the app's store page.
