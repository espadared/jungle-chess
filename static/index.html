How to play Jungle

The goal

Win by moving any one of your animals into the opponent's den (the square marked 🏠 at the far end), or by capturing every animal they have. If a player has no legal move left, they lose.

Moving

Every animal moves one square up, down, left or right. Never diagonally. You may never step into your own den.

Eating

An animal may eat an enemy animal of the same or lower rank:

🐘 8 Elephant > 🦁 7 Lion > 🐅 6 Tiger > 🐆 5 Leopard > 🐺 4 Wolf > 🐕 3 Dog > 🐈 2 Cat > 🐁 1 Rat

The one twist: the rat can eat the elephant (it runs up the trunk), but the elephant can never eat the rat.

The river

Only the rat may swim. A rat in the water cannot eat anything on land, and nothing on land can reach it while it swims - but a rat in the water can eat another rat in the water.

The lion and tiger can leap right over the river, straight across or straight up and down, landing on the far bank and eating whatever is there. They cannot leap if any rat, yours or theirs, is sitting in the water they would cross.

Traps ⚠

Three squares around each den are traps. What they do depends on the rule you picked in the menu:

  • Classic - an animal in the enemy's trap drops to rank 0, so any enemy piece can eat it. Your own traps do nothing to you.
  • Open traps - an animal on any trap, including its own, can be eaten by anything.
  • Safe traps - an animal on any trap cannot be eaten at all. All three squares beside a den are traps, so an enemy who lands on one is untouchable and walks in next move - but you can park your own animals there first, where they are equally untouchable and block the way. Games run long and turn on who claims those squares.
  • Home refuge - your own traps make your animals safe from capture, while the enemy's traps still strip your rank. Games run long, and defending is genuinely possible.

Draws

The same position three times, or 100 moves with nothing captured, is a draw. This mostly matters in the slower trap rules.
